## Proposed issues (could not be opened from the agent session: please open them)

1. **GD · Greedy card answers should cost more in the Nordic Fjord (F4).** E.g. stronger delayed bills on the greedy client answers (crypto farm, autoplay, deepfake ads), or a Nordic-specific `contextModifiers` on reputation. Then add a `min-maxer` strategy (balanced build, greedy answers) to `engine/strategies.ts` and a sim rule "balanced beats min-maxer".
2. **GD · Plant choice should move impact more (F5).** Options: raise the `carbon` weight in `SUSTAINABILITY_WEIGHTS`, or a small Responsibility drift tied to fossil share. Needs an engine owner (`engine/scoring.ts`).
3. **DEV · Analytics for the valley card and badges.** Track whether the "Why?" answer is revealed and which badges are earned (`run_end` payload), to see on `/dashboard` what players learn.
4. **ART-4 · Badge art.** Badges reuse the 7 category icons; a badge frame would make them more shareable (and could go on the share card).
